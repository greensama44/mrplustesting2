Is Mega Rewards really free?

Yes. Free costs $0 and needs no card. You get 6,000+ discounts to browse, 1x ticket in every draw, free giveaways every week, free Mega Tipping and your own dashboard. The one thing Free doesn't get is member prices.

What do I get with Plus?

Plus is $19.99 a month for one person, with member prices, 4–8% off gift cards, $23 mobile plans, 2-for-1 dining and 2x points.

How do the draws work?

Every draw is certified. Mega Daily is drawn every day at 6pm ACST with 10 winners of $100 each. Mega Friday is the big weekly draw. Every entry earns points.

What is the Mega Catch?

One product a day at a member price. Everyone can buy it, and Plus members pay less. It resets every day.

How do points and levels work?

Earn points on entries, check-ins, referrals and store buys. Level up from Rookie to Fan, Legend and Hall of Fame for bonus tickets. Spend points on tickets, merch, store credit or a free month of Plus.

Is Mega Tipping free?

Yes, every round of the season. Five codes, one account, and the best overall record wins $2,500.

Can I cancel anytime?

Plus is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime. Annual billing saves 15%.
